    Section B — Geographic Data Limitation (Technical)
    
      
    
        ⓘ
        
          Raw UNKNOWN is a technical LinkedIn export limitation, not a business failure.
          LinkedIn's bulk export does not include location or country. Geographic confidence will always be low.
          The business dashboard uses Opportunity Bucket V5 — classifying contacts by company, title, persona, and language — instead of showing UNKNOWN as the main conclusion.
